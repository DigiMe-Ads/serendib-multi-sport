02 — What we can learn from IRONMAN Goa

More than a race. A destination.

Destination

Goa is sold as a tourism experience, not only a start list.

Race week

Athlete check-in, expo, merchandise, Q&As, community activity and sponsor activations.

Belonging

Repeat participation, and athletes who feel part of an IRONMAN community.

Sponsors inside

Partners are part of the event experience, not logos on posts.

Colombo should build a full event ecosystem around race week.
