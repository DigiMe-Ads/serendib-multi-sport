13 — Measuring success

How we'll know it worked.

Each phase of the rollout has its own job, so each is judged on its own numbers. Targets are set with Serendib once 2026 baselines are shared.

Awareness

Reach and views of the hero film, press coverage, and growth in follower numbers across Sri Lanka and the region.

Oct–Nov

Community

Club and gym partners signed, training-session turnout and athlete-shared content.

Dec–Mar

Registrations

Sign-ups against the 2026 race, share of international athletes, and first-time athletes.

Apr–Jul

Partner content

Views, saves and shares on each partner series, collab-post reach and takeover engagement.

Tourism

Race-week hotel stays and travel booked through partners, plus reach of "Race the Island" content.

Race week
