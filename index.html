01 — The main idea

Not a normal yearly event. A comeback.

2026 proved it

IRONMAN can return to Colombo successfully. The race, the course and the crowds are now proof, not a promise.

2027 goes bigger

The aim is an event that feels much bigger, louder and more visible across Sri Lanka and the region.

One direction

Everything we make points to one line: Sri Lanka is back on the IRONMAN map.

04 — Make Colombo part of the campaign

IRONMAN + Colombo + Sri Lanka, together.

Port City, the waterfront, the skyline, sunrise, the coastline and Colombo's energy become the campaign's backdrop.

Recurring series · Race the Island

What international athletes can do before and after race day: hotels, food, travel, recovery and local experiences.

Who this creates value for

  • Hotels — race-week stays and packages
  • Airlines — international athlete arrivals
  • Tourism partners — pre- and post-race itineraries
  • Travel brands — the island as part of the trip

09 — Partner content in the calendar

One shoot. Every channel. All year.

From 2026 footage to a partner showcase

The best 2026 footage of athletes, the race village, crowds, products in use and the finish line is cut into a showcase film: “This Is What IRONMAN Looks Like in Colombo.” It then becomes a content system, not a single video.

  • Full showcase film on YouTube and LinkedIn, ending on “Be part of the next chapter”
  • 15–30 sec cut-downs for Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook
  • Collab posts co-published on Serendib's and each partner's accounts
  • Partner story takeovers during race week
  • A post-race recap reel for each partner

When partner content lands

  • Oct–Nov · The Return
    “Welcome to the team” partner reveal posts and teasers
  • Dec–Mar · Road to Race Day
    Hydration, recovery and gear tips built into training content
  • Apr–Jul · Build desire, push registrations
    Travel, stay and registration series; activation reveals
  • August · Own the month
    Live race-day content, takeovers and partner recaps

12 — How we work

Our monthly workflow.

The same five steps, every month, from October to August.

Step 1Plan

Monthly content calendar and shot list, matched to the rollout phase. Approved by Serendib.

Step 2Shoot

Planned shoots of athletes, training, Colombo locations and partners, with our team on site.

Step 3Review

Edited reels, films and posts shared for feedback and sign-off before anything goes live.

Step 4Publish

Posting, partner collab posts, community replies and paid boosts where agreed.

Step 5Report

Monthly results report, with what we'll adjust next month.

On the ground at key moments

Our team attends in person to capture content and cover live, subject to Serendib's requirements and approval.
